Get a YouTube Shorts transcript

Paste a public /shorts/VIDEO_ID link into the YouTube Transcript tool. It reads the caption track already exposed by YouTube, then gives you timestamps, copy, TXT and SRT downloads. It cannot create a track when the Short has none.

  1. Copy the Short URL from YouTube's Share menu or address bar.
  2. Paste it into the transcript tool and choose a caption language when more than one is available.
  3. Replay important cues, then copy the timed text or download TXT/SRT.

Important boundary: this page reads speech captions. It does not scrape comments, the written description, or words burned into video frames.

Why a Shorts link can fail

/shorts/ identifies a layout, not caption availability. A Short may have captions disabled, captions still generating, a private or removed status, or a browser bot check that a strict extension prevents. Retry once; if the track remains unavailable, use a saved file with Video to Text when you have permission to process it.

What you can export

  • Timed text keeps the source start times for replay and fact-checking.
  • Plain text removes time markers for notes and search.
  • TXT is a UTF-8 document; SRT is a numbered subtitle file for editors and players.

Timestamps come from YouTube's caption data, not from an estimate based on the Short's duration. Check proper nouns, numbers, rapid cuts, and speech over music before publishing a quote. The output has no speaker labels and does not read text visible in a frame.

Shorts review checklist

Replay every important cue after jumping to it. Check the first and last words around a cut, verify names against the audio, and select the correct language track when one exists. Keep the source URL beside the exported file so corrections stay traceable.

Privacy and processing boundary

This site asks YouTube which caption tracks the video has and may fetch the default track on the server. Successful lookup responses, including available caption text, may be cached for one hour. When a selected track needs a browser request, your browser downloads it directly from YouTube and may perform a Google bot check. No video audio is uploaded or transcribed. TXT and SRT exports are created in your browser. For a saved file, decoding and speech recognition run on your device through Video to Text; arbitrary video URLs are not transcribed server-side.

FAQ

Does this transcribe every Short from audio?

No. It reads an existing caption track. A saved Short without captions needs the local file workflow.

Can I use a normal YouTube URL?

Yes. Shorts paths, watch?v=... links and youtu.be/... links are accepted by the underlying transcript tool.

Is this a copy of on-screen text?

No. It is caption text for speech. Text printed inside frames needs visual inspection or OCR.

Search captions in InstaScript

Use YouTube Transcript or YouTube Transcript Download to search a loaded caption track, move between matches, and locate its caption line. Source time links open YouTube in a new tab. Search is read-only and does not change TXT/SRT exports. This guide does not run a separate caption engine.
