The direct answer

To generate captions, open the Instagram Transcript tool, paste a public Instagram Reel or video URL, or upload a saved MP4, MOV, WebM, MP3, WAV, M4A, or OGG file. InstaScript recognizes English speech in your browser and gives you timestamped text plus TXT, SRT and VTT downloads.

  1. Choose a public URL that works without a login, or select a file you have permission to process.
  2. Wait for the browser model to create speech segments.
  3. Replay important timestamps, edit wording in your subtitle editor, then export SRT for a player or TXT for notes.

Scope: this generates a speech-based caption draft. It does not add animated styling, read text printed in frames, translate speech, or identify speakers.

URL or local file

A public Instagram URL is useful when its media can be fetched without a login. Private, removed, region-limited, expired, or protected links can fail; save a copy you are allowed to use and upload it instead. Uploads are limited to 100 MB and are processed on your device.

Review before export

  • Replay words covered by music, effects, echo, clipping, or crosstalk.
  • Check accents, names, numbers, product terms, and URLs against the recording.
  • Split long cues and remove overlaps in an editor when a viewer needs more time to read.
  • Text shown on screen needs visual inspection or OCR; speech recognition cannot see video frames.
  • The current browser model is English-focused, so non-English output needs full manual review.

FAQ

Does it create burned-in captions?

No. It creates TXT, SRT and VTT files. A video editor can style or burn those captions into a video later.

Does it copy the written Instagram post caption?

No. It transcribes spoken audio. Copy the written post description separately when you have access and permission.

Is my uploaded video stored?

Uploaded media is decoded and transcribed in your browser. A public URL needs a temporary media fetch before local processing; the transcript is not saved as an account record.

Is InstaScript affiliated with Instagram?

No. InstaScript is independent of Instagram and Meta.

Export your edited transcript

In the shared transcription tool, Copy and TXT use your edited words. They are plain text by default; enable Include timestamps in TXT and Copy to add available start times. This option does not run speech recognition again. SRT, VTT and Open in Subtitle Editor require non-empty cue text and complete valid start/end times. Missing end times are not estimated. If timing is incomplete, keep the recording for review and copy or download TXT instead.
