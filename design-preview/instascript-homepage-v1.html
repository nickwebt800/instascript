How an Instagram transcript generator works

Instagram to text means turning spoken words in a public Reel or uploaded recording into readable text with timestamps.

Start with a public link or saved file

Paste a public Instagram Reel or video post URL, or choose a video or audio file from your device.

Speech recognition runs in the page

After the media is available, the browser processes the audio and returns a transcript when the format is supported.

Export the result

Copy clean text for notes, or download TXT and SRT files for editing and captions.

No account is required

There is no registration flow or transcript history to manage.

Answers before you start

FAQ

Can I make an Instagram Reels transcript from a public link?

Yes. Paste a public Instagram Reel or video post URL above. The page fetches the media link while speech recognition runs in your browser.

Does InstaScript upload my files?

Uploaded files are processed in the browser. The privacy message and the current processing behavior should remain visible near the tool.

Can I download subtitles?

Yes. The transcript output supports copying text and downloading TXT or SRT.

What file types are supported?

MP4, MOV, WebM, MP3, WAV, M4A and OGG are supported up to the current file-size limit.
