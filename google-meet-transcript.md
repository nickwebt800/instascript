## 1. Check which source is available

Google documents a native transcript feature for eligible accounts and configurations; consult [Google Meet transcript help](https://support.google.com/meet/answer/12849897?hl=en) for current availability and access. If you already have a native transcript, review that text. InstaScript does not retrieve private Drive documents, join meetings or provide a meeting bot.

**Check:** Your chosen source is available to you with appropriate permission.

## 2. Use a recording only for the interval it covers

For an existing local recording, use [Video to Text](https://instascript.app/video-to-text) for browser-decodable English speech up to 100 MB. Model download and language checks apply. Document when recording began and any interruptions. Local cue time starts at this file, not the meeting wall clock.

**Check:** An absent opening or interrupted section has not become an inferred transcript.

## 3. Separate evidence from meeting conclusions

Replay a proposed decision and the responses before writing that it was agreed. Keep speaker attribution, action ownership and your summary as checked manual notes. No automatic minutes or decisions are generated here. Save the transcript and notes separately.

**Check:** The notes cite the available source and explicitly preserve missing coverage.

## More questions and limits

### What processing and privacy boundaries apply?

Local decoding, recognition, replay, edits and downloads run in your browser. Model resources need network access, but the local recording and transcript are not uploaded. There is no account history or saved cloud project; download your work before closing the tab. Same-origin editor transfer keeps the cues and recording local between tabs. Instagram URL retrieval and YouTube caption lookup have separate server-assisted privacy boundaries.

### Where should I continue?

[Open the matching tool](https://instascript.app/video-to-text) and follow its current input requirements. Keep the original, review your result and download it before closing the tab.
