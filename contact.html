Contact

Have a question, found a bug, or want to request a feature? You can reach out in a few ways:

Email

Mail is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to the maintainer's inbox.

Send a message to contact@instascript.app and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days.

Please use this address for anything about the site — bug reports, privacy and data requests, ad or legal enquiries. It is the only mailbox we publish.

What to Include

  • If you found a bug: the URL you were on, the file or public Instagram link you used, and what happened (error message, blank output, etc.)
  • If you want a new feature: describe what you'd like InstaScript to do that it currently can't
  • If you have a privacy concern: mention "Privacy" in the subject line

Response Time

InstaScript is a personal project maintained by one person. We read every message but may not reply to all. Bug reports and privacy concerns get priority.
