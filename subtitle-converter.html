3. Validate the whole artifact in its destination

The SRT to VTT tool rejects an invalid numbered block rather than silently exporting a partial file; adjacent overlaps produce a warning. Some other routes keep usable cues, so inspect omitted material. Compare cue count, first/final range and key passages in the target player. Converting does not repair an offset or progressive drift; use Subtitle Editor to edit timing.

Check: Keep the original, immediate conversion and reviewed version as separate files.

More questions and limits

What processing and privacy boundaries apply?

The linked subtitle tools read, convert and export locally without uploading subtitle contents or imposing a speech-language limit. No cloud project is saved. They do not transcribe audio or automatically translate; save your work before closing the tab.

Where should I continue?

Open the matching tool and follow its current input requirements. Keep the original, review your result and download it before closing the tab.
