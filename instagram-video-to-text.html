The short answer

To convert an Instagram video to text, open the Instagram Transcript tool and paste a public Instagram URL, or upload a saved MP4, MOV or WebM file. Click Get my Reel transcript to transcribe spoken audio in your browser, keep timestamped segments, and copy or download TXT or SRT.

  1. Choose Paste Reel URL for a public video that opens without a login, or choose Upload video or audio for a saved copy.
  2. Wait for the media to load and for the browser speech model to process the audio.
  3. Review the timestamps, then copy the transcript or download TXT for notes and SRT for subtitles.

Important: this converts spoken audio to text. It does not extract the written Instagram caption, comments, or text burned into the video.

Public Instagram URL or saved video?

Use a public URL when the post can be opened without an Instagram login and its media can be reached by the fetch path. The page needs the URL only to obtain the public media; speech recognition starts in your browser after the media is available.

Upload a saved file when the post is private, deleted, login-gated, region-restricted, or the URL returns a page without playable media. The upload path accepts MP4, MOV and WebM files up to 100 MB. Use only a copy you have permission to process.

A correct-looking link can still fail because a session is required, the media address has expired, or the response has no audio track. A saved file bypasses that URL-specific failure, but a silent video still has no spoken words to transcribe.

TXT is best for searching, editing, quoting, and notes. SRT adds numbered subtitle cues with start and end times for a compatible editor or player. The SRT is made from recognized speech segments; it is not Instagram's original caption track.

Where processing happens

For an uploaded file, the page decodes the media and runs the English speech model on your device. The file is not placed in an upload queue, and the transcript is not stored as an account record. For a public URL, the site has to fetch the public media first, so that network step is different from the local upload path.

The browser model works in short audio passes. The first visit can take longer because the model must load; later runs can reuse the cached model. The privacy boundary and current limits are described on the privacy page.

Check the transcript before using it

Music, sound effects, fast speech, accents, names, numbers, and overlapping speakers can change the output. Replay the relevant timestamp before publishing a quote or using the words as an official record. Speech recognition supports English only. Detected non-English speech is rejected; an uncertain or failed language check stops processing with a separate error and creates no transcript.

FAQ

Can I convert a private Instagram video from its URL?

No. The public URL path needs media available without a login. If you are allowed to use the video, save it and upload the local file instead.

Does this read text written in the Reel?

No. It recognizes spoken audio. Post captions, comments, and text displayed inside the frames are separate content.

Which file should I download?

Choose TXT for a plain transcript and SRT when you need timestamped subtitles in a compatible editor or player.

Is InstaScript affiliated with Instagram?

No. InstaScript is an independent project and is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.

Keep permission, privacy, and copyright in mind when saving or republishing an Instagram video or its transcript.
