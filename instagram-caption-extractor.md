# Instagram Caption Extractor: Caption Text vs Transcript

> Separate a written Instagram post caption from spoken video text, then verify the transcript before exporting TXT or SRT.

Canonical: <https://instascript.app/instagram-caption-extractor>

## The short answer

An Instagram caption can mean the post description, spoken words, or text visible in a video. InstaScript handles spoken audio: paste a public URL or upload a saved MP4, MOV or WebM file, then review and download the result as TXT or SRT.

It does not scrape the written post caption, comments, hashtags, or text burned into frames.

## Three kinds of Instagram text

| Text | Location | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Post caption | Description below the post | Read or copy the post where you have access |
| Spoken transcript | Audio track | Replay important timestamps after transcription |
| On-screen words | Video pixels | Inspect the frame or use a separate OCR workflow |

Keep the source type in your notes. Hashtags in a post description may never be spoken, and a speaker may add details not present in the description.

## Extract and verify spoken text

Use a public URL only when its media opens without a login and can be fetched. For private, removed, region-limited, or expired links, upload a saved file you are allowed to process. Files up to 100 MB can be MP4, MOV, or WebM.

Replay every important segment. Check names, numbers, URLs, product terms, and words spoken over music. Mark uncertain words instead of silently correcting them.

```text
[00:00] The caption says one thing
[00:04] but the speaker adds a second detail
[00:09] so I keep both sources in my notes
```

TXT is useful for searching and notes. SRT adds numbered, timestamped cues for a compatible editor or player; it is not Instagram's original post caption.

## Accuracy checklist

- Music and sound effects can hide syllables.
- Overlapping speakers need manual speaker labels.
- Accents, names, and numbers need an audio check.
- The English-focused model makes non-English output a draft.
- Text visible in frames needs visual inspection or OCR.

## Processing boundary

Uploaded media is decoded and transcribed on your device. A public URL needs a network fetch first, then speech recognition runs in the browser. Use only media you have permission to save and process.

## FAQ

### Can this copy the written Instagram post caption?

No. Copy that description from the post itself when you are allowed to access it.

### Can it read words displayed in a Reel?

No. Text in frames requires visual inspection or OCR; this transcript covers speech.

### What should I keep after extraction?

Keep the original media, TXT or SRT output, and a note of the timestamps you reviewed so corrections remain traceable.
