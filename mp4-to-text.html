MP4 to text: the direct answer

MP4 to text means transcribing the spoken audio in an MP4 video into searchable words. Choose one local file above, press Transcribe this file, and review the timestamped result. Copy it, download .txt for notes, or download .srt for timed subtitle cues.

This tool reads the video's audio track. It does not turn a filename, title, comments or visible on-screen words into a transcript, and it does not fetch an MP4 from a video platform link.

How to transcribe an MP4

  1. Choose one file. MP4, MOV and WebM files up to 100 MB are accepted.
  2. Let the browser load the model. The first run downloads the speech model; later runs can reuse its cache.
  3. Review and export. Replay names, numbers and short words at their timestamps, then copy TXT or download SRT.

Example output

[00:00] Thanks for watching the product update
[00:06] The next release needs one final check
[00:13] We will publish the notes tomorrow

TXT is easy to search. SRT keeps numbered cues and time ranges. Neither format identifies a speaker unless the speaker says their name.

Privacy and review limits

The selected MP4 is decoded and transcribed in your browser. There is no account, transcript history or server upload in this local-file workflow. Keep the tab open until you save the result.

The model is intended for English speech. Music, room echo, clipping, rapid cuts and overlapping voices can change words or cue boundaries. It does not translate, label speakers, read text on screen or add caption styling.

FAQ

Can I paste a video link?

No. Save an MP4 you are allowed to use and choose the local file. This page does not fetch protected or private video URLs.

Does the MP4 leave my device?

No for this local-file workflow. The speech model runs in the browser and the page does not keep a server copy.

Can I use the result as captions?

Yes. Download SRT, then inspect cue breaks in your subtitle editor. The export contains speech timing, not a video preview or styling.

Why is the first run slower?

The first run downloads and initializes the speech model. A later file can use the cached model.

Cancel and retry

Only one transcription task can run at a time. Cancel processing discards its result; a model download or local audio/model operation already in progress may need to finish first. After a failure or cancellation, Try the same input again reuses the selected file in this tab. Selecting a different input clears that retry target. Local file contents are not uploaded. Model loading and speech recognition do not have a fixed timeout.

Review and edit your transcript

After transcription, replay the original recording with the player and click a timestamp to seek to a cue. Edit the words in each cue; Copy, TXT, SRT and VTT use your edits. When every cue has complete valid timing, choose Open in Subtitle Editor to change timing in another tab. Recording playback depends on your browser codec support. Subtitle Editor receives your transcript and recording locally between same-origin tabs; no project is saved.

Export your edited transcript

In the shared transcription tool, Copy and TXT use your edited words. They are plain text by default; enable Include timestamps in TXT and Copy to add available start times. This option does not run speech recognition again. SRT, VTT and Open in Subtitle Editor require non-empty cue text and complete valid start/end times. Missing end times are not estimated. If timing is incomplete, keep the recording for review and copy or download TXT instead.
