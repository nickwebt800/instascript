How to get a Vimeo transcript

  1. Open the Vimeo recording and use its download option, if the owner has enabled one.
  2. Save the file on your device. Do not download or process a video you are not allowed to use.
  3. Open Video to Text, select the file, and wait for the timestamped result.
  4. Copy the text, or download .txt for notes and .srt for subtitle editors.

What this Vimeo transcript workflow can and cannot do

The Vimeo page is only the source of a file. InstaScript does not log in to Vimeo, bypass privacy controls, or fetch a private link. Once you select a permitted local file, speech recognition runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded for transcription and no transcript is stored by this site.

  • Works with browser-readable MP4, MOV and WebM files up to the tool's 100 MB upload limit.
  • Returns spoken English with segment timestamps, plus TXT and SRT export.
  • Does not transcribe music, comments, descriptions, speaker labels or text shown only in the picture.
  • Noise, room echo, overlapping speakers and strong accents need a manual review.

Why a saved file is the reliable route

A Vimeo URL can point to a private showcase, an embed, or a player that requires a session. Treating every link as downloadable would either fail unpredictably or encourage access outside the owner's settings. A saved file gives you a clear permission boundary and lets the browser decode the exact media you selected.

FAQ

Can I paste a Vimeo URL here?

No. This page does not fetch Vimeo URLs. Download a file using Vimeo's own controls when available, then use Video to Text.

Is the video uploaded?

No. The selected file stays in the browser while the speech model runs. See the privacy policy for the processing boundary.

Can it make subtitles?

Yes. The result includes segment timestamps and an SRT download. Review the cues before publishing, especially where speakers overlap or music covers speech.

Does it support every language?

The included model is English-focused. Non-English speech may be incomplete or incorrect.
