The direct answer

To turn a Reel into text, open the Instagram Transcript tool and paste a public Reel URL, or upload a saved MP4, MOV or WebM file. It recognizes spoken audio in your browser and provides timestamped text plus TXT and SRT downloads.

  1. Choose Paste URL for a public Reel, or Upload File for a saved copy.
  2. Let the media load and the browser speech model finish.
  3. Check the timestamps, then copy the text or download TXT/SRT.

Scope: this is a speech transcript. It does not extract captions, comments, hashtags, or words printed inside the video frames.

Public Reel URL or saved file?

Use a public URL when it opens without a login and its media is reachable. Use a saved file for private, deleted, region-limited, or login-gated Reels. A link can still fail if its media address expired or the video has no audio; a silent file has no speech to recognize.

TXT is useful for searching and editing. SRT adds numbered start/end cues for subtitle editors and players. It is generated from recognized speech, not Instagram's original caption track.

Processing boundary

Uploaded media is decoded and transcribed on your device. A public URL is fetched first, then the browser handles speech recognition. The first run can be slower while the model downloads; later runs may use its cache.

Review before publishing

Music, effects, accents, names, numbers, fast speech, and overlapping speakers can change the result. Replay important timestamps. Non-English speech is a draft with the current English-focused browser model.

FAQ

Can I use a private Reel URL?

No. Upload a saved copy you have permission to process.

Does it read words shown on screen?

No. It recognizes spoken audio; use OCR for text in the frames.

Which download should I choose?

Choose TXT for plain text and SRT for timestamped subtitles.

Is InstaScript affiliated with Instagram?

No. InstaScript is independent of Instagram and Meta.
