The direct answer

To turn a Reel into text, open the Instagram Transcript tool and paste a public Reel URL, or upload a saved MP4, MOV or WebM file. Click Get my Reel transcript to recognize spoken audio in your browser and provide timestamped text plus TXT, SRT and VTT downloads.

  1. Choose Paste Reel URL for a public Reel, or Upload video or audio for a saved copy.
  2. Let the media load and the browser speech model finish.
  3. Check the timestamps, then copy the text or download TXT/SRT/VTT.

Scope: this is a speech transcript. It does not extract captions, comments, hashtags, or words printed inside the video frames.

Public Reel URL or saved file?

Use a public URL when it opens without a login and its media is reachable. Use a saved file for private, deleted, region-limited, or login-gated Reels. A link can still fail if its media address expired or the video has no audio; a silent file has no speech to recognize.

Timestamped output

[00:00] Open the Reel transcript tool
[00:04] paste a public link or select the saved file
[00:10] review the words before you publish them

TXT is useful for searching and editing. SRT adds numbered start/end cues for subtitle editors and players. It is generated from recognized speech, not Instagram's original caption track.

Processing boundary

Uploaded media is decoded and transcribed on your device. A public URL is fetched first, then the browser handles speech recognition. The first run can be slower while the model downloads; later runs may use its cache.

Review before publishing

Music, effects, accents, names, numbers, fast speech, and overlapping speakers can change the result. Replay important timestamps. Speech recognition supports English only. Detected non-English speech is rejected; an uncertain or failed language check stops processing with a separate error and creates no transcript.

FAQ

Can I use a private Reel URL?

No. Upload a saved copy you have permission to process.

Does it read words shown on screen?

No. It recognizes spoken audio; use OCR for text in the frames.

Which download should I choose?

Choose TXT for plain text and SRT for timestamped subtitles.

Is InstaScript affiliated with Instagram?

No. InstaScript is independent of Instagram and Meta.

Export your edited transcript

In the shared transcription tool, Copy and TXT use your edited words. They are plain text by default; enable Include timestamps in TXT and Copy to add available start times. This option does not run speech recognition again. SRT, VTT and Open in Subtitle Editor require non-empty cue text and complete valid start/end times. Missing end times are not estimated. If timing is incomplete, keep the recording for review and copy or download TXT instead.
