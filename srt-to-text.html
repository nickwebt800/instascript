SRT to TXT: keep the words, remove subtitle timing

For an SRT to TXT file, choose Convert to plain text, review the editable result, then select Download TXT. This uses the same converter above; it does not need a separate upload or account.

The TXT output keeps cue text and line breaks in source order. It removes cue numbers, time ranges and subtitle markup; it does not summarize, translate, label speakers or preserve timing for a video player. Keep the original SRT if you may need timed subtitles later.

How conversion works

The browser finds each subtitle cue, removes its sequence number and start/end time, and keeps the spoken text and line breaks. Cue text is joined in reading order. Common VTT markup tags are removed from the plain-text result.

If the pasted content has no usable SRT or VTT cues, the page reports that it could not find subtitle text so you can correct the source.

FAQ

Can I convert SRT to text without uploading the file?

Yes. Paste the SRT text or open one local SRT file. Reading and conversion happen in this browser.

Does this also work as a VTT to text converter?

Yes. Paste or open a VTT file and choose Convert to plain text. WEBVTT headers, cue numbers, and timecodes are left out of the result.

Can I edit the plain text before downloading?

Yes. The result is editable. Make any changes, then copy the text or download it as a TXT file.

Can I convert several subtitle files at once?

No. This page intentionally handles one subtitle file at a time so the workflow stays local and easy to review.
