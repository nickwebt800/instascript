Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Data We Collect

InstaScript runs entirely in your browser. When you upload a file for transcription, the audio is processed locally on your device using in-browser AI. Your uploaded files are never sent to or stored on any server.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for a name, an email address or a phone number. The transcript you generate stays in your browser tab and is discarded when you close it — we never see it. InstaScript does not run its own analytics, tracking or session-recording scripts. Basic traffic measurement, if any, comes from our host and is described in section 3.

2. URL-Based Transcription

When you paste a public Instagram URL — the only link type supported today — our server fetches the video on your behalf (to bypass browser CORS restrictions) and delivers it to your browser. The video is not stored on our servers — it is streamed through and discarded. Audio extraction and transcription happen in your browser.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Hugging Face CDN — delivers the AI speech recognition model to your browser on first use (~40 MB, cached afterward).
  • Cloudflare — hosts this website and provides CDN delivery. Cloudflare also measures traffic to this site on our behalf (aggregate page views and referrers), and may set cookies needed for security and bot protection.

4. Advertising

This website may display advertisements from third-party advertising networks (for example Google AdSense or another ad network). These partners may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, and they may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you view on this site.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not pass any file you upload or any transcript you generate to an advertising partner. Use of advertising technologies is governed by the respective ad network's privacy policy.

5. Cookies

InstaScript does not set cookies of its own. Our host, Cloudflare, may set cookies required for security and bot protection, and third-party advertising partners may set cookies to deliver and measure ads. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or you want to ask what data we hold or have it deleted, email contact@instascript.app or visit our contact page.
