How to get a Twitter video transcript

Save a video you are allowed to process from the post, then open Video to Text and choose the file. InstaScript decodes the audio in your browser and produces spoken words with timestamps plus TXT and SRT downloads. A post URL by itself is not a transcript input here.

  1. Keep the post URL beside the saved file so the result remains traceable.
  2. Upload the local MP4, MOV or WebM file and wait for browser transcription.
  3. Replay important cues, correct names or numbers, then copy text or download TXT/SRT.

Scope: the transcript is spoken audio. It does not include the post text, replies, usernames, music metadata, or words printed inside video frames.

Why a Twitter or X link can fail

Public visibility does not make a media URL a stable file endpoint. A post can be deleted, protected, age-gated, rate-limited, changed after reposting, or served through a login and bot check. X may also expose a page without exposing a downloadable video to a browser tool. Treat a failed URL lookup as an access boundary, not as evidence that the video has no speech. Use a saved file only when you have permission to do so.

What the transcript contains

  • Timed view: short segments retain start times for replay and fact-checking.
  • Plain text: words without time markers for notes and search.
  • TXT: a UTF-8 text document for quotes and editing notes.
  • SRT: numbered cues for subtitle editors and compatible players.

Timing follows the browser's speech segments; it is not a guess based on the post duration. The output has no reliable speaker labels and cannot recover words masked by loud music or clipped audio.

Verification pass for short social clips

Replay every quote at its timestamp. Check proper nouns, handles, numbers, profanity, rapid cuts and overlapping speakers against the audio. Mark uncertain words instead of silently filling them in. If the clip switches languages, verify the selected language separately. Compare any on-screen headline with the frame itself because it is outside the spoken transcript.

Privacy and processing boundary

After you choose a local file, decoding and speech recognition run in your browser. The transcript is assembled for the current page and is not an account record. InstaScript does not fetch arbitrary X URLs server-side or promise access to protected, private or removed posts.

FAQ

Can I paste an x.com or twitter.com URL?

Not for direct transcription. Save a permitted copy first, then use the local file workflow.

Does this copy the post caption or replies?

No. It transcribes spoken audio only. Post text, replies and text burned into frames need separate review.

Why is the output imperfect?

Short clips often contain music, accents, crosstalk and jump cuts. Replay the timed cues and edit the exported SRT or TXT before publishing.
